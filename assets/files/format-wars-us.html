RESEARCH PARTICIPATION CONSENT

Format Wars: Teaching AI Platform Strategy Through Simulation

You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating this educational simulation game. This study is being conducted by researchers at Boise State University.

WHAT IS THIS STUDY ABOUT?

We are studying how simulation games can help students learn about AI platform strategy, including concepts like network effects, switching costs, and institutional constraints in emerging markets.

WHAT WILL I DO?

You will play the Format Wars simulation game as part of your normal class activities. If you consent to participate in the research, your gameplay data (decisions made, outcomes, time played) and your survey responses will be included in our analysis. No personally identifiable information is collected — your data is linked only to a random session ID.

IS THIS VOLUNTARY?

Yes. You will play the game regardless (it is a class activity), but your participation in the research component is entirely voluntary. Your decision will NOT affect your grade in any way.

WHAT DATA IS COLLECTED?

  • A random session ID (not linked to your name or student ID)
  • Your in-game decisions and outcomes
  • Your survey responses (if you complete the surveys)

No names, email addresses, or student IDs are collected by the game.

RISKS AND BENEFITS

Risks are minimal — no greater than normal classroom activities. There is no direct compensation. Your participation contributes to educational research on simulation-based learning.

CAN I WITHDRAW?

You may withdraw at any time by not completing surveys or by requesting your data be excluded.
