Make a Bot — discussion assignment

Purpose of the Assignment

The main purpose of this assignment is to enhance your understanding of strategic management concepts and to develop your skills in using AI tools effectively. In the current AI-driven world, the ability to create and refine AI-generated content is crucial. This exercise will help you become a better editor and critical thinker, preparing you for real-world applications of AI in a business context. By creating and evaluating AI outputs, you will deepen your understanding of strategic tools and frameworks introduced in Chapters 1 and 2, such as Porter's Five Forces, PESTLE analysis, SWOT analysis, and strategic leadership.

Week 1 Assignment

For this week's assignment, you need to create a bot that can perform a useful task based on the content from Chapters 1 and 2. Your bot should incorporate real-world data and information to enhance the analysis.

  • Examples of Useful Bots:

    1. Porter's Five Forces Analysis Bot: Performs a Porter's Five Forces analysis for any specified firm.
    2. PESTLE Analysis Bot: Conducts a PESTLE analysis for a chosen industry.
    3. SWOT Analysis Bot: Generates a SWOT analysis for a company.
    4. Study Guide Bot: Creates a study guide for the key concepts from Chapters 1 and 2, including definitions and examples.

    Objective: The objective of this assignment is to develop a useful one-shot prompt where the AI will perform a relevant task that reinforces the concepts from Chapters 1 and 2. This task will enhance your ability to create precise and effective prompts and will help you understand how to incorporate real-world knowledge into AI-generated content.

  1. Task Description:

    • Select a task for your bot that is based on the content from Chapters 1 and 2. Examples include a Porter's Five Forces analysis, PESTLE analysis, SWOT analysis, or creating a study guide.

  2. Creating a Useful Prompt:

    • Assign a role or persona to the AI.
    • Write your prompt clearly and specifically.
    • Provide step-by-step instructions to guide the AI through the analysis.
    • Include examples or templates within your prompt to demonstrate the expected output.
    • Suggest using variations to get multiple perspectives or thorough analyses.

  3. Incorporating Real-World Knowledge:

    • Use reliable data sources to inform your analysis. Since ChatGPT may not access certain websites directly, you may need to download, copy, and paste relevant data into your prompt.
    • Recommended data sources:
      • Pew Research: Pew Research
      • Boise State University Library Databases: Library Guide
        • ABI/INFORM Collection: Company reports and ProQuest Annual Reports Collection.
        • Mergent Intellect: Public and private company information.
        • Standard and Poor's NetAdvantage: Financial statements and ratios.
        • EDGAR: SEC company filings.
        • Company Websites: Annual reports and investor relations pages.
        • The Textbook itself

  4. Submission:

    • Upload the prompt and the AI-generated output to Canvas by Wesdnsay at Midnight.
    • Ensure the output is comprehensive and uses real data to support the analysis.

Peer Review and Feedback (Due Sunday)

Task: Respond to the AI-generated outputs and prompts of two other students by playing a game called "What's Interesting, What's Wrong, What's Missing."

Review Focus:

  • What's Interesting: Here you identify what is interesting. Think to yourself, "What worked well?" If you were an editor, which aspects would you keep? 
  • What's Wrong: Identify any inaccuracies, errors, or incorrect applications of strategic concepts in the AI-generated content. Point out where the analysis does not align with real-world data or accepted theories.
  • What's Missing: Highlight any gaps or missing elements in the analysis. This includes overlooked factors, incomplete sections, or missing data that would provide a more comprehensive understanding of the topic.

Recommendations: Provide constructive feedback and recommendations on how the student can adjust their prompt to improve the AI output. Offer specific suggestions for enhancing accuracy, completeness, consistency and/or the application of strategic concepts.

Grading Criteria:

  • Your feedback will be graded on its quality. Ensure your feedback is:
    • Developmental: Focus on helping your peer improve their work. Provide actionable suggestions.
    • Encouraging: Maintain a positive and supportive tone, even when pointing out errors or omissions.
    • Critical: Be honest and thorough in your evaluation. Your goal is to help your peer understand and correct any mistakes.
  • Failing to maintain an appropriate tone or provide meaningful feedback will result in a deduction of points. Remember, you are helping each other learn and gain a new skill.
