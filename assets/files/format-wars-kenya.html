RESEARCH PARTICIPATION CONSENT

This Format Wars simulation is part of a research study on simulation-based learning in management education conducted by researchers at Boise State University.

You will play the game as part of your normal class activities. If you consent to participate in the research, your gameplay data (decisions made, outcomes, time played) and your survey responses will be included in our analysis. No personally identifiable information is collected — your data is linked only to a random session ID.

What we collect:

  • Which platform you chose and what decisions you made each turn
  • Game outcome (win/loss) and final stats
  • Your survey responses (if you complete the survey)

Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time by not completing surveys or by requesting your data be excluded.

Questions? Contact the research team at shyde@boisestate.edu

1. How to Play

November 2022. ChatGPT just launched. You lead a Kenyan organization choosing an AI platform.

Pick a platform below, then face 12 turns of events. Read the scenario, choose a response. Every choice has trade-offs.

Tip: Green terms can be clicked for definitions. Stat labels can too.

2. How to Win

You must hit all four conditions to win:

AI Readiness ≥ 70%
How capable your org is with AI. Grows from smart investments. Drains if sovereignty is low.
Local Impact ≥ 50%
How much your AI benefits Kenya. Swahili support, rural access, healthcare, partnerships all help.
Sovereignty ≥ 20%
Keep data in Kenya. Below 25%: readiness drains -1/turn. Below 15%: drains -3/turn. Deadly.
Lock-in < 65%
Don't get trapped by your provider. Above 65%: price hikes drain your budget. Blocks victory.
You lose if: Budget hits KES 0M or AI Readiness drops to 0%. OpenAI is expanding into Kenya every turn. The clock is ticking.
Team Skill reduces costs on every choice (shown in green). The most expensive option isn't always the best.
