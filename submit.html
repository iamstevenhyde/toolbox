The Toolbox is a small, early publisher of AI classroom tools, and it is open to other faculty. Two ways in: send a tool you have already run with students, or ask for one you wish existed.

Submit a tool Request a tool

submit a tool

This is an open call to faculty. If you have a classroom idea that works, bring it, and I will help turn a rough version into something another instructor can pick up and run. I am starting with a small handful of contributors, so every submission is read by me.

What a tool page needs. Every card follows the same shell so an instructor can compare tools at a glance: what it is, what students do and walk away with, the platform it runs on, the time and level, the prep, how to run it step by step, how to debrief it, and a link to the bot, file, or repo. If you have only part of this, send what you have and we will fill in the rest together.

Credit. Your name goes on the card and on a short author page that lists your tools. You keep ownership of what you built.

Review. I read it, run it or have a colleague run it, and write back with changes or a yes. Tools that have been used with real students go first. Nothing goes live without your sign-off on the final card.

Art. The site shell stays the same for everyone. Each tool can have its own look inside it, as long as it does not get in the way of using it.

Design partners. If you would rather build something with me than send a finished tool, say so in the form and I will get back to you.
